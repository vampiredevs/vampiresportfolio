How I Work

I try to make my designs clear enough that another person can actually build them.

01 — Start with the player

What are they doing, why should they care, and how is this different from the last activity?

02 — Build the NPC + objective

Give the character a role, then connect their personality to an objective and reward that makes sense.

03 — Write the states

Cover the normal interaction plus cases like accepting, leaving, not having enough money, or already completing something.

04 — Make it less repetitive

I look for places where a quest can use exploration, collecting, services, choices or humor instead of another identical combat objective.
